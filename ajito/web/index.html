Two ways to a great day.

☀

The healthy route

Start at 100. Lose 1 point per focus percentage point below 60%, plus 10 for each missed habit.

max(0, 60 − focus %) + 10 × missed habits
ϟ

The productive route

Start at 100. Lose 1.5 points per focus percentage point below 70%, plus 3 per missed habit. Focus above 70% can offset these habit deductions.

max(0, 1.5 × (70 − focus %) + 3 × missed habits)

We choose whichever route gives you the higher score.

Then subtract these from that score:

Waking after 8:30 AM
0.2 points per late minute, capped at 15 points. No penalty for waking earlier.
Bedtime away from 11:30 PM
0.2 points per minute earlier or later, capped at 15 points. Anchored to the waking day's 11:30 PM, even after midnight.
Social media above 45 minutes
0.2 points per extra minute. No separate cap; the final score stops at zero.
Exercise, outside, conversation
Each “No” counts as one missed habit in both routes. More focus never removes the timing or social-media deductions.
Off day / special day
After input validation, this overrides the final score to 100. Agree with your crew on when to use it.
Efficiency & final score
Focus minutes ÷ waking minutes × 100. The score is clamped to 0–100 and rounded to one decimal for saving. Whole-minute inputs must each fit within your waking day, which must be less than 24 hours.
Streaks & logging
A saved score of 65 or higher extends a good streak; below 65 extends a bad streak. Changing sides resets it to one. One entry per person per date; submit dates in order. Diary text does not affect scoring.
